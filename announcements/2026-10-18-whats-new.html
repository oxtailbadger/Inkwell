Inkwell now lives at inkwell-reads.xyz, and a lot has changed since you last dropped by. Here’s the two-minute tour.
New

🎧 Podcasts have their own home

Paste a link from Apple Podcasts, Spotify (episodes and shows), and Inkwell files it under Podcasts automatically. Nothing to pick, nothing to tag. Songs and playlists stay put. Nods, Save, Read and Dismiss all work the same.

📑 Tabs: All, Articles, Podcasts, Authors

All is the default: everything your friends shared, newest first, podcasts marked with a headphones icon. Want just one kind? Tap its tab. Authors got its own tab too, so the feed stays about your friends.

Paywalled posts are hidden, so you only see things you can actually read.

🔢 Signing in is easier

The sign-in email now includes a short code as well as the link. Asked for it on your laptop but opened the email on your phone? Type the code instead. The link only works in the browser that asked for it. Sharing from your phone while signed out no longer loses the link either.

✨ Little things

Inkwell catches double-shares and tells you who got there first, so go give them a Nod. The feed holds up no matter how much you’ve saved, read or dismissed.
